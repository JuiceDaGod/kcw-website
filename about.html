KCW brings together hall of famers, 30-year veterans, second-generation talent and high-flying newcomers from across the New England independent scene. Our yellow jacket mascot says it all: fists taped, stinger loaded, ready to swarm.

KCW Sunday Night Swarm, our weekly show, premieres Black Friday at 7 PM ET and then airs every Sunday at 7 PM ET on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, with Tony Jones calling the action and Commissioner John Lowe running the show.
